# Foundry DevX Agent Privacy Notice

Last updated: October 8, 2026

## Overview

Foundry DevX Agent is a read-only service used by the Microsoft Foundry
Developer Experience team to analyze activity in the Microsoft Foundry Discord
community. It helps identify unanswered questions, recurring developer issues,
engagement trends, and opportunities to improve community support,
documentation, and product experiences.

This notice describes the information processed by Foundry DevX Agent. Discord
separately controls information collected and retained through its services, as
described in the [Discord Privacy Policy](https://discord.com/privacy).

## Information the agent processes

The agent may process information available through the Discord permissions
assigned to it, including:

- Message and thread content
- Discord display names
- Message, channel, and thread identifiers
- Channel and thread names
- Message timestamps and reply relationships
- Reaction counts
- Aggregate server, channel, activity, and participation statistics

The agent processes only channels and threads that the Discord server has
authorized it to access. It is not designed to access direct messages.

## How information is used

Authorized Microsoft personnel may use the agent to:

- Identify unanswered or recurring technical questions
- Summarize community topics and trends
- Measure aggregate community activity
- Retrieve supporting examples for developer-experience analysis
- Prioritize documentation, product, support, and community improvements

The agent does not automatically post messages, moderate users, create support
cases, or make decisions about community members.

## AI-assisted processing

Foundry DevX Agent uses Microsoft Foundry and related Azure services to retrieve
relevant Discord messages and generate summaries or analysis in response to
questions from authorized Microsoft personnel. Results may include message
excerpts, display names, or references to source conversations so that findings
can be reviewed and verified.

AI-generated results may be incomplete or inaccurate and should be reviewed
before use.

## Storage and retention

Discord message data retrieved by the agent is cached in application memory for
up to five minutes to support a request and reduce repeated calls to Discord.
The agent does not maintain a separate permanent database of Discord messages.

Microsoft-hosted services used to operate, secure, monitor, and troubleshoot the
agent may process and retain service logs, diagnostic data, and generative AI
traces in accordance with their configured retention policies.

Deleting or editing content in Discord does not necessarily remove information
that was previously included in service logs, diagnostic records, or generated
analysis, subject to applicable retention requirements.

## Access and sharing

Access to the agent and its results is restricted to authorized Microsoft users
and service identities. Information may be processed by Discord and by
Microsoft-hosted services used to provide and operate the agent.

Information processed by the agent is not sold or used for advertising.

## Security and appropriate use

Microsoft uses technical and organizational measures intended to protect the
information processed by the agent. Community members should not submit
passwords, credentials, confidential business information, sensitive personal
information, or other secrets in Discord channels accessible to the agent.

## Privacy choices and questions

Discord provides controls for editing and deleting content and managing account
information. Those controls are governed by Discord's policies and the
permissions available in the relevant server.

For questions or requests concerning Microsoft's processing of personal data,
see the [Microsoft Privacy Statement](https://privacy.microsoft.com/privacystatement)
and [Microsoft Privacy Support](https://support.microsoft.com/contactus).

## Changes to this notice

Microsoft may update this notice when the agent's functionality, data practices,
or applicable requirements change. The date at the top of this notice identifies
the latest version.
